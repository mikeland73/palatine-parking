---
title: Palatine Smart Parking
---

An iOS and Android app for residents of the Palatine building to operate their
own assigned parking lift spot over Bluetooth.

The app talks directly to the controller in front of you over Bluetooth. It has no
accounts and no analytics or advertising; the only network traffic is anonymous
crash and diagnostic reports, described in the privacy policy.
